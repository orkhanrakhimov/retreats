Experience

Medicine, movement and hospitality

Medical Doctor

An MD with expertise in rehabilitation, injury prevention and first aid. She understands what is happening in your body, and how to move it safely.

Yoga & Pilates

A Yoga Alliance Level 3 instructor with over six years teaching Hatha, Ashtanga, Kundalini, Hot Power Pilates and Mat & Reformer Pilates, to groups and private clients.

Luxury hospitality

Years of private and group wellness sessions in luxury hospitality and professional fitness, including fitness events. Today she teaches at OkTopUs Fitness, one of Georgia's largest fitness clubs.

How she teaches

Yoga and Pilates, one approach: listen to the body

Hatha
Slow, steady postures and breath. The foundation of every retreat, and perfect for beginners.
Ashtanga
A dynamic, flowing practice that builds strength, heat and focus. Mornings, for those who want energy.
Kundalini
Breath, movement and meditation to release tension and calm the nervous system. Evenings, before deep sleep.
Hot Power Pilates
Strong, sculpting core work. Offered as an option on selected retreats.
Mat & Reformer Pilates
Precise, posture-focused strength work, and a big part of her rehabilitation approach for backs, hips and pelvic floor.
