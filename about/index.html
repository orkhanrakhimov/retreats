Medical doctor and yoga teacher

Anastasia Mzhavia

Anastasia has taught yoga and Pilates for more than six years: group classes, private clients and fitness events. Today she teaches at OkTopUs Fitness in Tbilisi and hosts her own retreats.

She studied medicine at BAU International University Batumi, with a focus on rehabilitation and injury prevention. She knows how the body works and adapts every exercise to your health and level.

Practice

What she teaches

  1. Hatha yoga

    Slow poses and breathing. Good for beginners.

  2. Ashtanga yoga

    A strong, dynamic practice that moves from pose to pose.

  3. Kundalini yoga

    Breathing, movement and meditation.

  4. Mat and Reformer Pilates

    Core strength, posture and a healthy back.

  5. Hot Power Pilates

    A fast, intense Pilates workout.

  6. Sound sessions

    Relaxation with Tibetan singing bowls and other sound instruments.
