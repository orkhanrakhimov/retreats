Anastasia Mzhavia

Medical doctor, yoga and Pilates teacher.

Anastasia has taught yoga and Pilates for more than six years: group classes, private clients, fitness events and luxury hotels. Today she teaches at OkTopUs Fitness in Tbilisi and hosts her own retreats.

She studied medicine at BAU International University Batumi, with a focus on rehabilitation and injury prevention. That is why her classes feel safe: she knows what is happening in your body and how to move it well.

@anastasiamzhavia Email Anastasia

What she teaches

  • Hatha yoga

    Slow, steady poses and breathing. Good for beginners.

  • Ashtanga yoga

    A strong, flowing practice for energy and focus.

  • Kundalini yoga

    Breath, movement and meditation to calm the mind.

  • Mat and Reformer Pilates

    Core strength, posture and a healthy back.

  • Hot Power Pilates

    An intense, sweaty Pilates workout.
