The retreats
Who are the retreats for?
Women who want yoga, Pilates, rest and to feel looked after: from complete beginners to experienced practitioners. Groups are small, up to 12 guests.
I've never done yoga or Pilates. Can I join?
Yes. Every class has gentler options, and Anastasia adapts the practice to each guest.
Can I come alone?
Most guests do. You can book a private room, or share a twin room and we'll pair you with another guest.

Health & safety
I have an injury or a health condition.
After booking you'll fill in a short health questionnaire. Anastasia is a medical doctor specialising in rehabilitation, so she'll plan modifications for you, or tell you honestly if a retreat isn't right for you yet.
Can I join if I'm pregnant?
[Anastasia's guidance on pregnancy]
Is there medical help on site?
Anastasia is a doctor with first-aid expertise and stays with the group throughout. For anything serious, we know the nearest clinic for every venue.
Can the kitchen handle my diet?
Yes. Tell us about allergies and preferences (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free) when you book.

Travel
How do I get to the venue?
A private transfer from Tbilisi is included in every retreat. The meeting point and time are on each retreat page.
I'm flying in from abroad.
Welcome! We'll help you plan your arrival and can recommend where to stay in Tbilisi before and after.
What should I pack?
You'll receive a packing list with your booking confirmation. Mats and props are provided.
