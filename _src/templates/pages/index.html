In class

More than stretching

Yoga and Pilates work on the whole body and the mind. A class with Anastasia brings the parts together, so you leave stronger, looser and calmer.

  • StrengthHolding poses and Pilates work build a strong core, back and legs.
  • FlexibilitySlow, safe stretching opens the hips, shoulders and spine.
  • BalanceStanding poses train stability, posture and focus.
  • BreathingBreath work calms the nervous system and steadies the mind.
  • RelaxationMeditation and rest to release tension and recover.
