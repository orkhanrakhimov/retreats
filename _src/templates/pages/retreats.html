The retreats

What to expect

A few days away from everyday life: good rest in a beautiful place, yoga and Pilates every day, and a teacher who is also a doctor.

  • Top places to stay

    We choose top-rated hotels and retreat places in quiet, beautiful locations: in the mountains, by the sea or in the countryside. Comfortable rooms and good beds, so you sleep well and recover.

  • Yoga and Pilates every day

    Classes for every level. Beginners get simple, safe options, and experienced students get more of a challenge.

  • Care from a doctor

    Before you arrive, Anastasia reads about your health and level. In class she adapts the exercises to injuries, back pain or anything else you've told her.

  • Time for yourself

    Free time between classes to rest, read, walk or explore the area.

  • On your own or with friends

    Many people come alone. You'll practise in a calm, friendly group with people of every level.

  • Everything clear before you book

    Each retreat page shows the place, dates, price, programme and what is and isn't included. Anastasia answers your questions personally.
