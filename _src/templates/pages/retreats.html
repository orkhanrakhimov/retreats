What every retreat includes

  • A beautiful place to stay

    Hand-picked boutique hotels with comfortable rooms and a spa, sauna or pool.

  • Yoga and Pilates every day

  • All meals

    Fresh local food from the hotel's kitchen. Tell us about any diet.

  • Transfers

    We pick you up and bring you back: from Tbilisi, or from the airport for retreats abroad.

  • A doctor on hand

    Anastasia reads your health form before you arrive and adapts the classes to you.
