The retreats
Who are the retreats for?
Women who want to rest, move and feel looked after: from complete beginners to experienced practitioners. Groups are small, up to 12 guests.
I've never done yoga. Can I join?
Yes. Every class has gentler options, and Anastasia adapts the practice to each guest.
Can I come alone?
Most guests do. You can book a private room, or share a twin room and we'll pair you with another guest.
