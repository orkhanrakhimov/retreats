Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and how you stay in control of it.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

retreats.ge is run by Anastasia Mzhavia in Tbilisi, Georgia. For anything about your data, write to {{ site.email }}.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through the forms on this site:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Which retreat or service you are interested in
  • Your experience with yoga or Pilates and what you would like to work on
  • Health information you give us, such as injuries, pain, conditions or pregnancy
  • For corporate enquiries: your company, team size and message

Why we collect it

To contact you about the retreat or session you asked about, and, as a medical doctor, so that Anastasia can plan classes that are safe for you. We do not use your information for anything else, and we never sell it.

Health information

Health details are sensitive. We only collect them with your explicit consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form. Only Anastasia reads them, and only to keep your practice safe. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Form submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet in our Google account, and Anastasia receives an email notification for each one. Google stores this data on our behalf as our service provider. Only Anastasia and the person who manages this website have access. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while they are useful for the retreats and sessions you are interested in. You can ask us to delete them at any time, and we will.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Write to {{ site.email }} and we will reply as soon as we can.

Visitor statistics

To understand how the site is used, we count page visits. For each visit we record the page, where the visitor came from (for example Instagram or Google), the type of device, and the time zone set in the browser. We do not use cookies for this, do not store IP addresses, and cannot identify you from it.

Other services

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and links to Instagram. When you use those, Google and Instagram process data under their own privacy policies.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
