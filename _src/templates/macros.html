I've never done yoga or Pilates. Can I come?

Yes. Classes are for every level, and Anastasia adapts the exercises to you.

I have an injury or back pain.

Tell us when you book. Anastasia is a medical doctor with a background in rehabilitation, so she'll adapt the classes for you, or tell you honestly if a retreat isn't right for you yet.

Can I come on my own?

Of course. You're welcome on your own or with friends.

How do I book and pay?

Register your interest on the Retreats page. Anastasia contacts you personally to confirm your place. Payment is by bank transfer.

What if I need to cancel?

[Cancellation policy]

How do I get there?

Travel to the venue isn't included, so you can drive or come the way that suits you. Each retreat page has the location and directions, and for retreats abroad, flights aren't included either.
