  • A beautiful place to stay

    Hand-picked boutique hotels with comfortable rooms and a spa, sauna or pool.

  • Yoga and Pilates every day

    Yoga in the morning, Pilates in the afternoon, both for all levels.

  • All meals

    Fresh Georgian food from the hotel's kitchen. Tell us about any diet.

  • Transfer from Tbilisi

    We pick you up in the city and bring you back.

  • A doctor on hand

  • Time for yourself

    Free hours every day to rest, walk or enjoy the spa.

Questions

I've never done yoga or Pilates. Can I come?

Yes. Classes are for all levels, and Anastasia shows an easier version of every exercise.

I have an injury or back pain.

Tell us when you book. Anastasia is a medical doctor with a background in rehabilitation, so she'll adapt the classes for you, or tell you honestly if a retreat isn't right for you yet.

Can I come on my own?

Yes, most women do. Take a private room, or share a twin room with another guest.

How do I book and pay?

Send a booking request from the retreat page. Anastasia replies personally and sends the details for a 30% deposit, which holds your place.

Is transport included?

Yes, a transfer from Tbilisi and back is included in every retreat.
